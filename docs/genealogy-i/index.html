The record

  • 104 individuals across 5 generation columns; 27 marriages; 80 parent–child links.
  • Transcribed from the plate and verified against it. Clan descent is matrilineal, which gives every bracket an independent check; all 27 marriages pass it.

Navigating this chart

  • A + at the start of a line marks a spouse, printed on the line below the person they married.
  • F. and M. give the sex of each person as printed.
  • The horizontal leader rule runs from a person to the bracket holding their children; the bracket itself hangs on the mother’s line.
  • A number after a name is the person’s age when the data was collected. d. means they had already died when Parsons recorded the genealogy, during her fieldwork of 1918–19; where she knew the year she gives it, as in d. 1913.
  • Every person number is a stable link — #p1 addresses person 1’s first printed line, and the address bar keeps it while you read.
  • The chart region can be focused and panned with the arrow keys; on a narrow screen, scroll sideways to follow the later generations.
  • With JavaScript: with the chart focused, press / to find a person by number or name; selecting a person number opens its register card, and Esc closes it. The Theme and Scale controls remember your choice.

Editorial notes

  • Clan membership is matrilineal: every sibling group carries its mother's clan. That rule was used to verify the bracket readings against the plate.
  • A dash in place of a name means the plate printed no name.
  • The dashed rule separating the two halves is an editorial addition, not on the plate. It marks the two founding couples, 1+2 and 54+55.
  • Person 8 (Yu˙si) appears twice on the plate — as husband in the upper half and as a son of 58+59 in the lower half. Here he is drawn once, in the lower half, with a cross-reference standing in for the repeated sibling group.
  • The ‘+’ line under 76 is numbered 68 on the plate but names Shuwaiʼᶦri, Turkey — person 67. The chart prints 68, as the plate does, and links it to 67; the misprint is recorded on union U23. Person 67’s own cross-reference — “For second wife and offspring see below, 76, 90-3” — is what identifies him.
  • The plate brackets 83, 84 and 85 under 68 alone. She has two husbands on the plate, 69 and 70, and the bracket does not say which marriage the children belong to — which is why the transcription records their father as unassigned. The chart draws them as the plate does, in one group under 68. In the register and the person cards this edition attributes 83 and 84 to 68’s marriage with 69, and 85 to her marriage with 70, marked †. That attribution is an editorial reading resting on documentary evidence from outside the plate. The plate does not state it, and the supporting records are not reproduced in this edition.
  • The generation ruler above the chart, the linked person numbers, and the register below the chart are editorial apparatus of this edition, not part of the plate.
